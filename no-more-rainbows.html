No More Rainbows?

Home / Plays / Play 1 of 6

© 2026 Corliss Parker. All rights reserved.

Alice, a retired psychologist and long-time activist, is determined to continue marching for environmental justice and human rights, in spite of a recent serious fall and impaired vision. While celebrating her mother’s seventy-fifth birthday, Lara pleads with Alice to stay home and skip a demonstration she helped organize. ICE agents have arrived in their city and are searching for a family they love. Lara fears her mother will be hurt and might fall again. Alice’s lover, daughter, grand-daughter, and several spirits offer advice and testimony. Alice must decide how she will respond to potential and real threats to herself, her friends, and the democracy she loves.
