Ocean’s Inn

Home / Plays / Play 5 of 6

© 2026 Corliss Parker. All rights reserved.

Carole and Simon, former lovers now in their late fifties, meet by chance on an Upper West Side Manhattan street undergoing extreme gentrification. They reminisce about the neighborhood they once loved and lost, the overabundance of gaudy, glossy shops and sterile banks, and their lost relationship. They laugh, cry, rehash old wounds, embrace, and reconsider their futures.
