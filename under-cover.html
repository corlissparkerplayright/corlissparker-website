Under Cover

Home / Plays / Play 6 of 6

© 2026 Corliss Parker. All rights reserved.

Sophie, both as a ten-year-old and a twenty-two-year-old, stages and describes Duck and Cover, the Civil Defense action propagated and practiced in the United States during the Cold War. Interactions among members of a deeply religious, conservative, military family are described. Conversations between Sophie and her parents and her Roman Catholic teachers about faith, obedience and miracles are staged. Ultimately, twenty-two-year-old Sophie celebrates a fragile independence from past troubled relationships.
