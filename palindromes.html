Palindromes

Home / Plays / Play 2 of 6

© 2026 Corliss Parker. All rights reserved.

Katy, a busy New York City therapist and mother of teens, receives a frantic, middle-of-the-night call from her brother in December, 2001. Matt has gotten an eviction notice, and he is desperate. He has lived in THE TEXAS ARMS, a trailer park in a suburb near Austin, for years. Old anxieties and symptoms linked to an earlier experience of homelessness following his chaotic return from Vietnam, resurface. Matt tells Katy he will move into a cave in the Hill Country. She immediately flies to Austin, sees and feeds Matt, and speaks with their parents and administrators in the Veterans Association and other agencies. After engaging in a scuffle with the manager of Matt’s trailer park, she offers Matt a housing option he may or may not accept.

PALINDROMES received a staged reading in September, 2025, at the Emerging Artists Theatre’s SPARK Festival in NYC.
