About

Corliss Parker is a playwright and short story writer whose work examines the human condition in modern times. She explores how people experience joy, love, loss, fear and confusion in the contexts of current planetary upheavals, human migrations, emerging advanced technologies, extreme climate events, political chaos and wars. While striving to represent the humor, pathos, and tragedy in the human experiment, she often reminds herself of Samuel Beckett’s words: “Try again. Fail again. Fail better.”

Corliss has been writing plays and short stories for three years. She worked many decades as a clinical psychologist, a therapist, and a professor of psychology. It was an awesome, rewarding, challenging profession. She is not an award-winning playwright, though she imagines what that might feel like. She has had several staged readings in New York City and was a finalist in the Don McCann Memorial One-Act Playwriting Contest in 2025. Writing is the gift that contains and eases Corliss’s sadness and despair over human beings’ terrifying capacity to hurt one another. Creating and documenting truths gives her hope in the human capacity to love and repair and care for one another.
