Saint James

Home / Plays / Play 3 of 6

© 2026 Corliss Parker. All rights reserved.

Two teens — one a climate change believer and activist, the other a climate change skeptic — spar during a Climate Club meeting in their midwestern high school. Kat is slightly injured and mocked on social media when Matt knocks her poster over. She refuses to return to school. Later, with the help of Abe Brooks, their principal, and Sandy Miller, a retired school psychologist, the teens learn to listen to and empathize with each other. Views about environmental injustice, and evangelical theories and scientific evidence of climate change are explored.

SAINT JAMES was a first runner-up in the Don McCann Memorial One-Act Playwriting Festival in 2025.
